# Business Insights

## Key Findings

### 1. Revenue Performance
The marketing campaigns generated significant revenue across multiple channels, with certain channels outperforming others.

### 2. ROI Analysis
ROI varies between channels, indicating that some marketing strategies are more cost-effective than others.

### 3. Cost Optimization
Campaigns with high costs but lower revenue should be reviewed to improve marketing efficiency.

### 4. Conversion Performance
Higher click volumes do not always result in higher conversions, highlighting the importance of optimizing landing pages and audience targeting.

### 5. Business Recommendation
- Invest more in high-ROI channels.
- Reduce spending on underperforming campaigns.
- Monitor campaign performance regularly using the Power BI dashboard.
- Use KPI metrics such as CPC, CPL, ROAS, and ROI for decision-making.
